# tools/kl-crosscheck · U-M1s-14 KL numeric-protocol equivalence cross-check

**msuiter in-house KL-NMF (`src/rust/engine/src/nmf.rs::fit_kl_with_init`) vs the R NMF package brunet algorithm**: a one-off fixed-platform numeric-protocol equivalence experiment (does not enter the CI gate; per `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §7 entry 1 / D11 / A8 and the U-M1s-14 row of `docs/ROADMAP.md` M1s). Acceptance criterion: with identical initialization and iteration count, `‖ΔW‖_F/‖W_R‖_F < 1e-10` (same for H) + element-wise relative difference of the objective trajectory < 1e-12.

**Verdict (2026-09-30, local machine)**: **PASS — numeric-protocol equivalence holds**. Endpoints (T=50/200) relative differences 0.9e-15 – 6.4e-15, 5 orders of magnitude below the 1e-10 criterion; all numbers are in `result.md` (auto-generated by the script, numbers recorded in full).

## Plan selection: A (external initialization run straight through the package entry point); B/C not needed

The brunet of NMF 0.28 is registered as `NMFStrategyIterative`: the `Update` slot = H step then W step, via the **C kernel** reached through `.Call("divergence_update_H/W", PACKAGE="NMF")` (not pure R, so the "diff the R source" route of plans B/C is neither usable nor necessary). The package entry `nmf()`'s `seed=` accepts an **NMF model object** (`nmfModel(rank, W=W0, H=H0)`) as external initialization — plan A holds. Cross-check validity is enforced by four mechanism probes (asserted automatically by the script):

1. **Injection fidelity**: the model returned with `maxIter=0` is bit-exact to the seed `W0/H0` under `identical()`;
2. **Exact control**: `maxIter=T` runs exactly T steps (`niter(r)==T`); `stopconv=1e6` disables the connectivity early stop;
3. **Determinism**: re-running with the same parameters is bit-exact identical;
4. Loop structure (read from the package source): `while(TRUE){stopFun(i); if(stop||i>=maxIter)break; i++; updateFun(i)}` ⇒ **`run(maxIter=t)` is a deterministic prefix of `run(maxIter=T)`** — the R-side per-t trajectory is collected by repeated runs with `maxIter=1..200` (~10 s per case); the prefix property is self-evidenced at file level on each side.

## Same initialization (gate-critical) and numeric exchange

All inputs (V/W0/H0) are quantized to **dyadic fractions (k/1024 grid) with 10 decimal fractional digits** (integer cases satisfy this naturally) and written to CSV as short exact decimal strings. Reason (found during the experiment): **on this platform R's decimal-string→double parse can be off by 1 ulp on a 17-significant-digit string** (e.g. `3.7393736185701556e+01`); 17-digit strings do not round-trip exactly, whereas with short exact expansions the parsers on both sides agree bit-exactly (measured over 2 ten-thousand (20000) samples + a per-case `identical()` round-trip assertion). Driver output uses `.17e` (R reading it back incurs ≤1 ulp, relative 2.2e-16, 4 orders of magnitude below the criterion, not a confounder). The init is drawn uniformly from {1/1024,…,1}×`sqrt(mean(V)/k)`, strictly positive (zero absorption impossible), `set.seed` 401/402/403 recorded on the record.

## Protocol differences on the record (all are findings; they do not affect the equivalence conclusion)

1. **brunet `pmax.inplace(·, 2.2e-16)` floor every 10 steps**: in case3 R pins 120 H/W entries at 2.22e-16 while ours decay freely down to 0–1e-113; the contribution to the relative F-norm is ≤1e-15 (the dominant term within the case3 endpoint 5.0e-15).
2. **ε-guard semantics**: on the case3 R trajectory min(WH)=1.34e-15 < our KL_EPS=1e-12 — the guard does participate, but the number of entries with "V>0 and WH<1e-12" = 0 (across 21 checkpoint states over the whole run), i.e. it fires only on V=0 entries (r is 0 on both sides), **benign**.
3. **All-zero rows**: R `nmf()` input validation rejects them outright; our engine accepts them (structural zeros are handled by the epsilon policy).
4. **All-zero columns**: the R brunet C kernel yields NaN propagation from 0/0, and a zero-exposure sample makes its own connectivity check crash ("non-conformable arrays"); our engine stays finite. Hence case3 substitutes a "low-count sample (total 3)" for a dead column in the cross-check (a realistic low-TMB shape).
5. **R decimal-parse 1-ulp error** (above); the experiment sidesteps it with dyadic-quantized inputs and records it.
