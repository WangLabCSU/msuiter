## 1. Executive verdict

1. sigminer's lag vs SigProfiler is on three axes, not cosmetics: compute kernel (R `NMF` package call chain), decision science (K arbitration, refit selection, QC sentries), and catalog currency (bundled COSMIC v3.4 vs upstream default v3.6).
2. Headline measured gap: on msuiter's own pinned-hardware M7 table, sigminer CRAN 2.3.1 extraction ran 830-1609 s wall vs SigProfiler 36-40 s on identical harness cells (20-45x), CPU-seconds 1089-2197 vs ~86 — compute-bound, consistent with the NMF kernel being the bottleneck (`bench/results/docker_m7_degraded_20261010-202330/timings_sharded_master.csv`).
3. The kernel axis has an upstream answer already staged: sigminer issue #478 (OPEN) names `omicverse/rust-NMF` — bit-equivalent to R `NMF` brunet/lee/offset/nsNMF, 76-280x with NNDSVD init (`gh repo view omicverse/rust-NMF`). msuiter independently validated its own KL kernel against R NMF brunet at ‖ΔW‖F/‖W‖F ~ 1e-15 (ROADMAP M1s entry `docs/ROADMAP.md:99-102`), so the cross-check tooling exists to certify such a swap.
4. The quality axis is subtler than the speed axis: the `bp_*` chain is functional but ships no CV-calibrated K arbitration (NMF cophenetic/silhouette family), no likelihood/LRT refit selection (QP/NNLS/SA only), no artifact/contamination sentries, no GMM normalization — all present in SigProfiler's stack and all already delivered, certified, and calibrated inside msuiter (M2/M3a/M3b/M4).
5. The catalog axis is a data/licensing gap, not an algorithm gap: D3 law keeps COSMIC numeric matrices out of every repo; the field-standard solution is pinning provenance to the BSD-2 SigProfilerAssignment mirror (`docs/research/05-standards-data.md:24,34-37`), with msuiter's refdb v1 (pinned `ff61b0f` mirror bundle, ROADMAP `docs/ROADMAP.md:165`) as the working precedent.
6. Integration plan: seven phases, one issue-tracked PR per phase, landing across `ShixiangWang/sigminer` (P0-P3, P5), `msuiter` (P4, P6 as engine-registry/bench PRs), and the campaign (P7, hardware-owned). Parity is defined as an acceptance gate, not a wish: regenerating the 42-cell M7 table with sigminer arms within 2x wall-clock of the sigprofiler arm and no TP-only judgment regression.
7. Explicit non-goals (section 6): no GPU NMF, no deep-learning/HDP extraction, no CN/SV refit migration into msuiter, no pre-measured speed claims, no unverified-paper QC machinery.

## 6. What NOT to do (YAGNI cuts)

1. Do not rewrite the extraction pipeline in R for speed; the kernel swap is the answer, pipeline rewrites re-open the witnessed-NAMESPACE can of worms (m7 adapter header lesson, `bench/m7/adapters/run_sigminer.R:12-19`).
2. Do not add GPU NMF to sigminer v3 — the campaign is CPU-pinned, maintenance cost is real, and the 2x gate is reachable without it (revisit at a G3 review only if the 2x gate fails on CPU).
3. Do not move CN/SV work toward msuiter — hard v1.x non-goal (`docs/ROADMAP.md:252`); keep CN/SV development where the witnessed users are (sigminer), and simply log the SigProfiler CN/SV catch-up drift (G12) for the quarterly review.
4. Do not adopt substSignature-/SigLDA-style QC as new machinery in this window: those papers were not verifiable in this environment's academic databases (section 7) and cosine-similarity QC is already superseded by the calibrated `ms_compare` nulls for the gates that matter; re-open if the primary sources become attested.
5. No deep-learning or HDP extraction paths (already ❌/⏳ in CAPABILITY-MATRIX engine matrix `docs/CAPABILITY-MATRIX.md:78`; corpus concurrence `docs/research/00-executive-summary.md:25` "no credible winner").
6. No vendoring of COSMIC numeric matrices into any repo (D3; mirror-pinned provenance only).
7. No speed claims in NEWS/README before the P6 measured table lands — zero-fabrication policy as modeled by `docs/PERFORMANCE.md` ("a figure there would be fabricated", `docs/PERFORMANCE.md:77-80`).
8. Do not chase mSigSDK/mSigPortal — zero registry hits, existence unattested (`docs/research/07-algorithm-zoo.md:17`).
